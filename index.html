Solar Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026
Applies to Solar Player, Android package com.solarplayer.app.v01.

1. Who provides this app and how to contact us

Solar Player is maintained by the developer using the GitHub account SwailumZafar. For support or privacy enquiries, use https://github.com/SwailumZafar/solar-player-privacy/issues/new or the developer contact email displayed on the Solar Player Google Play listing. GitHub issues are public: do not include private media, contacts, vault credentials, device identifiers, or other sensitive information. Use the Play listing's developer email for a confidential enquiry. Opening GitHub or another external page is optional and that service applies its own privacy terms.

2. Local playback and information on your device

Solar Player reads audio and video files and their metadata when you permit media access. This supports your library, thumbnails, folders, sorting, playlists, search, playback and editing. Playback history, recent positions, favourites, search history, playlists, preferences and widget state are stored locally. Audio and video are played on your device. Solar Player has no developer-operated account, advertising system, cloud media library or analytics backend. It does not automatically upload your library to the developer.

3. Solar Share and information you choose to send

Solar Share discovers nearby devices and transfers items you select, such as audio, videos, photos chosen with the system picker, documents, accessible app packages, and contacts. The receiving user is shown a file offer and must accept it. Devices exchange the selected file contents and information needed for the transfer, including names, types, sizes, integrity checks and device or peer names. Pair only with a device you trust and confirm the displayed authentication information. Recipients can retain and redistribute files after receipt.
If you choose contact sharing and grant Contacts permission, the app reads contact names and details so you can select contacts. Selected contacts are exported to temporary vCard files and sent to your chosen recipient. Temporary contact exports older than 24 hours are removed on a later export; Android may reclaim cache earlier. File and app-package exports also use temporary local cache files. Contacts and installed-app lists are not automatically uploaded to the developer.
Received files are saved to shared device storage. Transfer records, received-file catalogues and interrupted-transfer checkpoints are stored locally. A nearby device may see your advertised device name and connection information while discovery or sharing is active. Network addressing and temporary Wi-Fi credentials are used for local connections; the app does not use them to determine your geographic position.

4. Google Play services Nearby

The optional Nearby transport uses Google's Google Play services Nearby SDK. Google documents collection of connection performance information and device information, including connection reliability, latency, throughput, device model, country, Android build version and application package name, to improve the service. This is separate from the files you select for peer-to-peer transfer. Google processes this information under its own privacy practices. Device settings can allow or deny usage and diagnostics collection, usually under Settings > Google > Usage & diagnostics; the route may vary by device. See https://developers.google.cn/nearby/connections/overview?hl=en and https://policies.google.com/privacy. LAN/QR sharing is also available without the Nearby transport. Solar Player itself does not operate a remote diagnostics server.

5. Permissions and optional features

Audio and video permissions let you browse and play local media. Legacy storage permissions support older Android versions. Media-management access, where you allow it, supports file editing, trash and restoration through Android. Nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth permissions support discovery, pairing and transfers. On Android 12L and older, Android requires location permission for some nearby-radio and hotspot operations; Solar Player does not request GPS coordinates or use this permission for location tracking. Contacts permission is requested only for contact selection. Camera access is used when you choose to scan a pairing QR code, not for background recording. Notifications and foreground services keep playback or an active transfer visible; wake and Wi-Fi locks help user-started operations finish. Biometric authentication is performed by Android: Solar Player receives the authentication outcome and never receives a fingerprint template. You can deny or revoke optional permissions in Android Settings, though the associated feature may stop working.

6. Private Vault and security

Vault files are encrypted locally using authenticated AES-GCM encryption. Vault credentials are protected using a PIN-derived key and Android Keystore; biometric access uses Android's authentication system. The app can restrict screen capture and lock the vault when backgrounded according to your preferences. Transfer connections use the selected transport's encryption and authentication mechanisms. No security method is an absolute guarantee. Losing the vault PIN or local keys, clearing app storage, or uninstalling can permanently remove access to vault contents. Restore or export needed files before removing the app. Deleting files does not guarantee physical secure erasure from flash storage or independent backups.

7. Retention and deletion

Local history, preferences, playlists, transfer records and vault files remain until removed through the relevant app controls, Android Clear storage, or uninstallation. History can be cleared in the app. Android manages trashed public media and its retention. Clearing a transfer history does not delete received public files. Public originals, restored files and files saved to shared storage can remain after uninstallation; delete those separately in your file manager or gallery. Copies held by recipients are controlled by those recipients. Temporary caches are subject to app cleanup and Android storage management. For data handled by Google or GitHub, use that provider's controls or contact mechanisms. Solar Player has no server account to delete and cannot remotely erase files it does not possess.

8. Children and other services

Solar Player is a general-purpose media utility and is not designed specifically for children. It provides no content catalogue, social feed, advertising or in-app purchasing. Parents and guardians should supervise the media and files children access or share. Google Play services, Android system components and websites you independently open have their own privacy practices.

9. Changes

Updates to this policy will be dated and provided in the app and on its public privacy-policy page. A change in features or third-party services can require updated disclosures.
